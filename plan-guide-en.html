Program Overview

This is a three-month progressive training roadmap built for beginners. Each month raises the intensity slightly so you can build a consistent habit, master form, and get stronger without burning out.

Training Principles

1. Progressive Overload

  • Month 1 · Foundation: learn movement patterns, build base endurance.
  • Month 2 · Intensify: add sets, reps, and new variations.
  • Month 3 · Consolidate & Push: challenge yourself with higher density work.

2. Weekly Frequency

  • 3–4 sessions/week: Monday, Wednesday, Friday are mandatory; Saturday is optional full-body mobility + cardio.
  • Session length: 30–45 minutes. Short, focused, and repeatable.
  • Recovery: Tuesday/Thursday/Sunday are complete rest days.

3. Body-Part Split

  • Monday: Chest + Arms (push muscles)
  • Wednesday: Back + Core (pull + trunk)
  • Friday: Legs (lower body strength)
  • Saturday: Optional light full-body session

Month 1 · Foundation Phase

Goals

  • Build muscular endurance
  • Lock in proper technique
  • Create a sustainable training rhythm

Training Style

  • 3 sets per movement, 8–15 reps
  • Rest 30–60 seconds
  • Form quality > load

Sample Week (Week 1)

Monday · Chest + Arms

  • Push-ups — 3 sets × 8–10 reps
  • Dumbbell Bench Press — 3 sets × 10–12 reps
  • Dumbbell Curls — 3 sets × 12–15 reps
  • Hammer Curls — 3 sets × 12–15 reps
  • Triceps Extensions — 3 sets × 12–15 reps

Total Time: 35 minutes

Wednesday · Back + Core

  • Dumbbell Rows — 3 sets × 10–12 reps
  • Bent-Over Reverse Flyes — 3 sets × 12–15 reps
  • Plank — 3 sets × 30 sec
  • Crunches — 3 sets × 15–20 reps
  • Russian Twists — 3 sets × 15 reps/side

Total Time: 35 minutes

Friday · Legs

  • Squats — 3 sets × 10–12 reps
  • Lunges — 3 sets × 10 reps/leg
  • Leg Curls — 3 sets × 12–15 reps
  • Calf Raises — 3 sets × 15–20 reps
  • Glute Bridges — 3 sets × 15–20 reps

Total Time: 35 minutes

Saturday (Optional) · Light Full Body

  • Jumping Jacks — 2 sets × 30 sec
  • High Knees — 2 sets × 30 sec
  • Push-ups — 2 sets × 8 reps
  • Bodyweight Squats — 2 sets × 10 reps
  • Plank — 2 sets × 30 sec

Total Time: 20 minutes

Month 2 · Intensification

We add more total work, introduce new core moves, and extend time under tension.

  • 4 sets for major lifts, 12–18 reps
  • Add push-up variations, side planks, and single-leg calf raises
  • Optional Saturday session now includes burpees

Month 3 · Consolidate & Push

The final month keeps the same structure but pushes reps into the 18–20 range, challenges your core with longer planks, and drives conditioning with higher-rep legs.

  • Focus on tempo control and breathing
  • If a movement feels too easy at 20 reps, add load or slow the eccentric
  • Quality sleep + nutrition become critical for recovery

Safety Checklist

  • Get medical clearance before starting a new program.
  • Warm up joints (neck/shoulders/hips/knees) for 5 minutes before training.
  • Stop immediately if you feel sharp pain or dizziness.
  • Log every workout; note pain, fatigue, or skipped sets.
  • Stretch or walk 5 minutes post-session to cool down.

Common Questions

Q: Can I train on consecutive days?
A: Preferably not. Keep at least one rest day between strength sessions so muscles rebuild properly.
Q: What if I only have 20 minutes?
A: Prioritize compound moves (squats, push-ups, rows). Do 2 rounds instead of 3 and move with intention.
Q: How do I know when to progress?
A: If you complete all prescribed reps with perfect form for two sessions in a row, add 2–3 reps or a bit of weight the next week.
